Project

Shipping a game means more than making features work. It has to be stable for players you'll never meet.

King of Cards was developed by a three-member team over roughly six months and released publicly on Steam.

My contribution

What I did

  • Scene development and setup, and implementation tasks to support game functionality and project requirements.
  • Manual testing throughout development: identifying bugs and supporting troubleshooting and resolution.
  • Refining gameplay scenes and resolving technical issues to improve stability and player experience before release.

Team

  • Three developers sharing design, development and release work.
